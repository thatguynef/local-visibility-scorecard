## Revenue opportunities to investigate (owner data required)

- **Search Console clicks by query cluster** — Public SERP observation shows where the business appears; Search Console shows whether those impressions produced clicks.
- **Search Console CTR and average position** — A ranking can be visible and still be ignored. CTR and position separate a ranking problem from a result-quality problem.
- **Priority services by gross profit** — The audit measures visibility across the configured services. The owner knows which services deserve disproportionate attention.
- **Qualified leads from non-branded organic search** — Visibility is not the same as qualified demand. Only the owner can show whether an organic visit became a real lead.
- **Revenue from organic discovery** — A visibility gap matters more when the affected service produces meaningful revenue.
- **AI referral traffic** — A mention in an AI answer may or may not produce a visit. Referral data is the only public-adjacent way to see the handoff.
- **AI-origin leads and jobs** — Being recommended by an AI system is not proof that the recommendation becomes a customer.
- **Revenue concentration by service and area** — A diversified visibility profile only matters if the underlying revenue is also diversified.
- **Paid discovery return** — Paid search and Local Services Ads are optional discovery channels. Their absence never lowers the public score.
